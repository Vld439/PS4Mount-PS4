Settings

A game only sees an add-on the system has installed: big ones are copied to the console's storage, in the background (a few minutes). Off, they are not used. On (the default): the .pkg files are moved into ps4/<title ID>/ on the drive, so the console's own installer does not find them again. Off: they are used where they are, anywhere on the drive, and are not renamed or moved. Pkgs sent to the console's storage are added from there, with no install to wait for. They still take internal space.
NAS (NFS or SMB) Not available on this firmware: there is no FUSE patch for it. Experimental on this firmware: its FUSE patch is itemzflow's and was not tried with PS4Mount (it was on 13.52 and 6.72). If the console freezes when the share is mounted, restart it and turn the NAS off. Firmware 5.05 to 13.52, and only with pkgs used where they are: the share is never renamed or moved. It needs a wired console or a good Wi-Fi: games read at the speed of the network.
How to share a folder from a computer or a NAS

Windows: right-click the folder, Properties, Sharing, Advanced Sharing, tick "Share this folder". Then fill in User and Password here with your Windows account. If the console does not find the PC, set its network as Private.

macOS: System Settings, General, Sharing: turn on File Sharing and add the folder. In Options, turn on SMB sharing for your user, and use that user and password here.

Linux: share the folder with Samba (read only, guest allowed) or NFS (/etc/exports), and let the console's address through the firewall.

NAS box (Synology, QNAP, a router with USB): turn on SMB file sharing in its settings and give the shared folder read access.

A cable is much faster than Wi-Fi. With no switch or free router port, a cable straight from the computer to the console works too: share the computer's internet connection on that cable.

Google Drive

Copies of your games in your own Google Drive: back up the library, send a game, and download it back to any drive of the console. PS4Mount can only see the files it puts in Drive (folder PS4Mount), nothing else of your Drive.

First, your own Google client (once). PS4Mount comes with none: yours stays on this console only.
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com, create a project and enable the Google Drive API.
  2. In Google Auth Platform (OAuth consent screen): audience External, and add your Google email in Audience → Test users (the app stays in Testing).
  3. In Clients (Credentials), create an OAuth client of type TVs and Limited Input devices, and copy its client ID and secret here.

Download links

The console downloads them itself (you can close this page): a pkg, a ZIP or a RAR, also in parts. Paste the direct links, the ones that start the download, one per line; all the parts of a RAR (name.part1.rar, name.part2.rar...) become one download. Its pkgs are added to the library when they arrive.
A link with no file name in it: add | and its name.
Not needed for one that opened another download, nor for the site in brackets at the start of the name ([SITE]-game): those are tried by themselves.
